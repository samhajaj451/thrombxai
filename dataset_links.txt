ThrombXAI — Dataset access links
=================================
Datasets used in the study, with their public access URLs.

Primary dataset
---------------
Name        : MIMIC-IV Waveform Database
Identifier  : mimic4wdb
Role        : ECG waveforms and vital signs used for training and evaluation
              (Methods Section IV.A; Reproducibility Statement)
Cohort      : 2,847 patients - 4,271 four-hour windows - 21.9% positive
              subject-level split 70/15/15 (1,993 / 427 / 427 patients)
Access      : PhysioNet credentialed access (PhysioNet Credentialed Health Data License)
URL         : https://physionet.org/content/mimic4wdb/

Supporting links
----------------
MIMIC-IV (parent clinical database) : https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/
PhysioNet (host)                    : https://physionet.org/

Notes
-----
The records are de-identified and credentialed; they are not redistributable. Obtain PhysioNet
credentials before downloading. Offline runs in this release use a deterministic synthetic cohort;
the real records load through the manifest path once placed under data/mimic4wdb (see
scripts/prepare_data.sh and docs/deviations.md).
